MP2026

Personal, self-hosted content publishing automation.

What this app is

MP2026 is a private, self-hosted tool operated by a single owner for personal use. It is not a public service and it does not serve third-party users.

What it does

Operated by its owner on the owner's own machine, MP2026:

  • Monitors YouTube channels that the owner owns and manages;
  • Uploads short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts) to those same owner-operated channels using the YouTube Data API v3, with the youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl scopes;
  • Redistributes the owner's own content to the owner's own Facebook and Telegram accounts and channels.

All activity is limited to accounts and channels owned by the operator. The app does not access, collect, or process data belonging to any other person.

Privacy

We do not sell or share any data. OAuth credentials are stored encrypted on the operator's own machine. See the full Privacy Policy.
